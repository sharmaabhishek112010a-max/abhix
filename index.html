About the app

An AI tutor built to help you understand

Abhix Study AI is the Android app from Abhix AI. It brings an AI tutor and your everyday study tools together in one place, organised around subjects and chapters from Nursery to Class 12 for your chosen curriculum, grade and region.

Chat in General AI, Study or Practice mode, get answers in English or Hinglish, choose a Dark, Light or System theme, and keep your chats, notes, quizzes, flashcards and plans organised in the app.

How to use

Start in four simple steps

  1. Install

    Download the APK from this page and install it on Android 7.0 or newer.

  2. Set up

    Choose your region, curriculum, grade, AI mode and response language in Settings.

  3. Pick a tool

    Open the AI tutor, Solver, Subjects, Quizzes, Flashcards, Notes, Planner or Analytics.

  4. Revise

    Save notes, retake quizzes and flip flashcards. Verify important facts with your textbook.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy, clearly explained

Last updated:

Google Sign-In is optional

You can use Google Sign-In, but you don't have to. Some features may require you to sign in. If you sign in, your Google account is used to sign you in and may be used to set up your profile in the app.

AI requests go to an external AI provider

When you use AI features, your prompts and relevant conversation context (including any image you attach to a message) may be sent to the selected external AI provider to generate a response. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in AI chats.

Study data is stored locally

Your study data in the app, such as chats, notes, quizzes, flashcards, study plans and settings, is stored locally on your device.

Android backup

Android backup is allowed for the app. Eligible data may be backed up depending on your device and Google settings.

Account deletion

How account deletion works, and whether local data is deleted together with an account, has not been decided yet. We won't guess here; this section will be updated once it is final. Questions? Email .

Pre-registration email

Permissions

The app asks for Internet and network-state access, which its AI features and Google Sign-In need.

Security and accuracy

We never publish API keys, tokens, passwords or signing credentials on this website. AI answers can be wrong, so please verify important facts with your textbook or teacher.
